Twenty players, one arena, last one standing wins. Every round a random player becomes the Wordsmith and types a word — METEOR, FLOOD, ZOMBIES — and the word becomes real as a hazard that hits everyone, caster included. Learn stronger words by surviving or grabbing glowing scrolls. And mind the zone: outside it, you burn.
